Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. About CurioByte

CurioByte Shorts is a video creation project focused on educational content about science, nature, space, and interesting facts.

2. Google and YouTube Data

CurioByte uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API to upload approved videos to an authorized YouTube channel.

The application requests the youtube.upload permission to upload videos. It does not request the user's Google password.

3. Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally during authorization and may be stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets for automated processing.

Video approval status and related metadata may be processed through GitHub Actions and Telegram.

4. Third-Party Services

CurioByte uses third-party services including Google, YouTube, GitHub, Telegram, and Pixabay.

These services have their own privacy policies and data processing practices.

5. Data Sharing

Data is transmitted to service providers as necessary to generate videos, process approvals, and upload approved content.

6. Revoking Access

Users may revoke Google account access at Google Account Connections.

To request deletion of application-held data or credentials, contact the CurioByte operator.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: bytecurio438@gmail.com

8. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application or its services.
