About CurioByte

CurioByte is a short-form educational video project producing engaging facts and discoveries in English for YouTube Shorts and other supported platforms.

What We Explore

🪐 Space

Planets, stars and extraordinary astronomical facts.

🐙 Nature

Surprising discoveries about animals and our planet.

🧠 Science

Interesting scientific explanations and everyday curiosities.

How the App Works

The CurioByte application assists its operator with creating educational videos, submitting them for review through Telegram and uploading approved videos to the operator's authorized YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

YouTube authorization is performed through Google OAuth. The application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
